Bird identification: BirdNET

Bird and animal sounds are identified with the BirdNET models by the K. Lisa Yang Center for Conservation Bioacoustics, Cornell Lab of Ornithology, and Chemnitz University of Technology (birdnet.cornell.edu).

  • Models (model.json, group1-shard1of13.bin to group1-shard13of13.bin, area-model.json, group1-shard1of2.bin, group1-shard2of2.bin, en_us.txt): used under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), the licence stated by the BirdNET-Analyzer project. The BirdNET Live project distributes them under CC BY-SA 4.0; this site follows the stricter, non-commercial terms. The model files are redistributed unmodified, as published in TensorFlow.js format by BirdNET Live. The only change is that the location model's model.json was renamed to area-model.json so all files can sit in one folder.
  • Engine code (birdnet-worker.js, audio-processor.js): from BirdNET Live, Copyright (c) 2025 BirdNET-Team, MIT License. birdnet-worker.js was modified: flat file paths, and a compact detect message that returns only likely species.
  • Citation: Kahl, S., Wood, C. M., Eibl, M., & Klinck, H. (2021). BirdNET: A deep learning solution for avian diversity monitoring. Ecological Informatics, 61, 101236.
